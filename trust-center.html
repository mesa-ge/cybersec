01

What you can send us to check

If something feels off, it's worth checking. Here's the kind of thing people send us every day.

Suspicious SMS, WhatsApp & email

Unexpected texts or messages pushing you to click, pay, or 'confirm' your details.

Dubious websites & online shops

A shop or site that looks too cheap, too new, or asks for payment in unusual ways.

Investment & crypto 'opportunities'

'Guaranteed' returns, trading platforms, or coins a stranger or 'advisor' wants you to buy.

Romance & 'friend in trouble' scams

An online partner, or a 'relative' or 'friend', who suddenly needs money or gift cards.

Job-offer & rental scams

Jobs that ask for an upfront 'fee', or flats you must deposit on before ever seeing them.

Phishing links

Links that imitate your bank, a courier, or a login page to steal your password.

Fake delivery, bank & tax notices

'Pay a small fee', 'your parcel is held', or 'tax refund' messages that aren't real.

Suspicious phone calls

Callers claiming to be your bank, the police, or 'tech support' asking for access or codes.

Social-media impersonation

A fake profile using your name, your photos, or a brand you trust to fool others.

04

Where to report fraud in Cyprus

If you've been targeted or caught out, these are the people to contact. We'll point you to the right one for your situation.

Police — Cyber Crime

Report online fraud and cyber-crime to the Cyprus Police. In an emergency, or if money is actively moving, call 112.

Your bank or card provider

If you shared card details or made a payment, contact your bank immediately to freeze cards and try to stop transfers.

CySEC — investment scams

The Cyprus Securities and Exchange Commission handles fake brokers, 'investment advisors' and unlicensed platforms.

Not sure who to tell?

That's exactly what we're here for. Send us the case first and we'll tell you who to contact and what to say.

06

What this service is — and what it isn't

We want to be completely honest about what we can and can't do.

Informational help, not advice

Our verdicts are informational and best-effort. They are not legal or financial advice. For legal steps or money you've lost, speak to a lawyer, your bank, or the authorities.

No guarantees

Scammers change their methods constantly. We can't guarantee that every scam will be caught, or that a case we consider safe truly is. Always use your own judgement too.

You stay in control

We never ask for your passwords, PINs, or full card numbers, and we'll never move money for you. Any action on your accounts is yours to take.
