01

What we protect

A complete look at your digital life — then we fix what matters most, in plain language and at your pace.

Secure home network & Wi-Fi

We harden your router, replace default passwords and risky settings, set up a separate guest network and a VPN, and put your smart-home and IoT devices on a controlled, segmented network — so a cheap gadget can't become a way into your home.

Device protection & cleanup

We check your phones, laptops and tablets for malware, spyware and stalkerware, remove what we find, and look for hidden monitoring, suspicious profiles and configuration changes someone may have left behind.

Everyday digital hygiene

We turn on two-factor authentication everywhere it counts, set you up with a password manager you'll actually use, arrange encrypted backups of what you can't afford to lose, and add monitoring that warns you when your data appears in a breach.

Account & identity protection

We lock down your email, banking and social accounts against phishing and takeover, protect you against SIM-swap attacks on your phone number, and review your social-media privacy so strangers can't piece your life together.

Privacy & digital-footprint reduction

We run an OSINT self-check to show you exactly what's exposed about you online, then work to remove your details from data-broker and people-search sites and reduce what the internet quietly knows about you and your family.

Family & children safety

We set up parental controls and age-appropriate protections, secure your children's devices and new gadgets, and give you clear, practical guidance so the whole family is safer online — without turning your home into a surveillance state.

Travel & physical-digital opsec

Before you travel we prepare your devices for public Wi-Fi, airports and hotels, reduce your risk from device theft, and advise on border crossings and sensitive destinations so your data stays yours wherever you go.
